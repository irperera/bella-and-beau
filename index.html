Settling in

Give them time to adjust

Shy kittens need a little time to get used to a new home. These steps help most kittens feel safe in the first few weeks.

  • Start with one quiet roomFood, water, litter box and a bed in one place, then open up the house slowly.
  • Offer places to hideA box or covered bed lets them watch from somewhere they feel safe.
  • Let them come to youSit on the floor and wait. Shy kittens warm up faster when they choose to.
  • Play every dayWand toys are a great way to build trust with a playful but shy kitten.
